# DOWNSTREAM - Visual Treatment

Environmental thriller screenplay visual treatment featuring Ricky Burchell, Kurtis Blow, and Ayla Demirci.

## Logline
After losing his wife and faith, a small-town pastor joins a disgraced journalist to expose a chemical cover-up poisoning their community.

**Truth beneath:** Two people seek proof of God by forcing the world to tell the truth.
